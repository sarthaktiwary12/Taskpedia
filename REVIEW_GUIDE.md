# 🚀 Simple Review App - Quick Guide

## Access

**URL:** http://localhost:5004

## How It Works

### Card-Swiping Interface (Like Tinder for Tasks)

- One task shows at a time in a big card
- Shows: Task name, domain, number of subtasks
- You rate it, card swipes away, next task appears

### Rating System

**Press keyboard numbers OR click buttons:**

- **Press `5` or click ⭐ PREMIUM** - "Shut up and take my money" tasks
  - Example: Pick up broken glass, Make coffee, Transfer patient

- **Press `4` or click 🔥 HIGH** - Very valuable, clear customer demand
  - Example: Wash dishes, Mop floor, Stock shelves

- **Press `3` or click 👍 MEDIUM** - Useful but not exciting
  - Most tasks that are "okay" but not impressive

- **Press `2` or click 👎 LOW** - Marginal value
  - Very niche or limited market

- **Press `1` or click 🗑️ TRASH** - Delete these
  - Planning, monitoring, calibration, admin tasks

- **Press `Space` to SKIP** - Not sure, come back later

### Stats at Top

- See real-time counts of what you've rated
- See how many tasks remaining
- Progress bar at bottom

### Visual Feedback

- Good ratings (HIGH/PREMIUM) → card swipes RIGHT
- Bad ratings (LOW/TRASH) → card swipes LEFT
- Smooth animations

## Tips for Fast Review

1. **Use keyboard** - Much faster than clicking
   - Keep fingers on 1-2-3-4-5

2. **Go with gut feeling** - Don't overthink
   - Would a robotics company demo this? 5 or 4
   - Is it boring/admin work? 1 or 2
   - Unsure? 3

3. **Think about the question:**
   - "Would customers PAY for a robot that can do this?"
   - "Would this make a KILLER demo video?"
   - "Is this technically IMPRESSIVE?"

## What Makes a PREMIUM Task? (Press 5)

✅ **High market value** - Universal demand
✅ **Technically impressive** - Makes spectators say "wow"
✅ **Solves real problems** - Clear practical utility
✅ **Shows sophistication** - Advanced capability
✅ **Generalizable** - Skill transfers to other tasks

Examples:
- Pick up broken glass (delicate + dangerous + common)
- Make coffee (universal demand + marketable)
- Fold laundry (everyone hates it + technically hard)
- Transfer patient (healthcare critical + shows capability)

## What Gets TRASH? (Press 1)

❌ Planning/scheduling
❌ Monitoring/logging
❌ Calibration/setup
❌ Testing/debugging
❌ Documentation/reporting
❌ Abstract cognitive tasks

## Your Goal

Find the **100-200 PREMIUM tasks** that make robots:
- MARKETABLE to customers
- IMPRESSIVE in demos
- VALUABLE in real-world use

Don't worry about getting everything perfect - just rate quickly and honestly!
